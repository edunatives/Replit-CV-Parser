Job Purpose

The role is responsible for defining, leading, and executing the organization’s Research, Development,and Innovation (RDI) strategy, with a focus on emerging technologies and future-ready capabilities. The position drives innovation pipelines, manages technology experimentation environments, drive in-house developed solutions, and builds partnerships across the global ecosystem. It also leads enterprise-wide technology knowledge management initiatives, ensuring that critical insights, learnings, and technical intelligence are captured, shared, and embedded into collective organizational culture.

Job Responsibility

Define and lead the organization’s RDI and emerging technology strategy, ensuring alignment with long-term technology goals and digital transformation priorities.
Scout, assess, and prioritize disruptive and high-impact technologies, coordinating across units to identify strategic opportunities.
Oversee technology labs’ end-to-end operations, including ideation, prototyping, PoC development, testing, and solution validation.
Drive open innovation models, enabling co-development frameworks, joint ventures, startup collaborations, and agile pilot programs.
Build and manage partnerships with startups, academia, global research networks, and technology vendors to accelerate innovation.
Establish and govern enterprise-wide knowledge management frameworks for capturing, curating, and disseminating technical insights.
Promote the adoption of structured knowledge repositories, expert networks, and reusable learnings to enhance organizational intelligence.
Lead cross-functional teams in executing innovation programs, ensuring transparency, governance, and measurable business impact.
Champion a culture of experimentation, continuous learning, and safe-to-fail environments across the organization.
Ensure compliance with technology-related regulations, including data governance, cybersecurity, IP rights, and ethical standards.
Advise senior executives on emerging technology trends, investment opportunities, and strategic RDI directions.
Apply strong problem-solving skills to resolve complex technical and strategic challenges.
Maintain a solid technical understanding of emerging technologies, development practices, and innovation methodologies.

Job Responsibility (cont.)

Years Of Experience

8–12+ years of progressive experience in RDI, innovation management, research management,

emerging technologies, or related technical leadership roles.
